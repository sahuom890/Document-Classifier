The International Space Station orbits Earth at an altitude of approximately 400 kilometers, traveling at a speed of about 28,000 kilometers per hour. Astronauts aboard the ISS conduct important scientific experiments in microgravity conditions. These experiments cover a wide range of fields including biology, physics, and materials science. The station serves as a platform for observing Earth and studying the effects of long-duration spaceflight on the human body. NASA, together with international partners including ESA, JAXA, and Roscosmos, maintains the station and plans future missions to the Moon and Mars.
